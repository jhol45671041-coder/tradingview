How to use the calculators

A quick guide to getting accurate results from every tool.

Open Calculators from the menu and pick the tab you need — Pip Value for the value of one pip, Position Size for how big your trade should be, Profit / Loss for the result of a trade, Risk : Reward to check a setup before you take it, Margin and Leverage for your exposure, plus Compounding, Drawdown and a Currency Converter. Select your pair (forex majors and crosses, plus XAU/USD Gold and XAG/USD Silver under "Metals"), fill in the boxes, and results update instantly — you can also press Calculate. When you change the pair, the sample price updates automatically so the numbers stay realistic, but any price you type yourself is never overwritten. Key conventions: one standard forex lot is 100,000 units (JPY pairs use a pip size of 0.01), one lot of Gold is 100 troy ounces and one lot of Silver is 5,000 troy ounces. Cross pairs are converted to USD using reference rates. Always double-check your broker's contract specifications, and never risk more than 1–2% of your account on a single trade.
